Telehealth

Anywhere in Oregon, from wherever you actually are.

Not a lesser version of therapy.

For people working rotating shifts in departments where being seen walking into an office matters, telehealth is often the only version that's realistic. It also means you aren't burning an hour of drive time on either side of a session.

  1. Book a free consultation

    Thirty minutes, no cost. You ask whatever you need to ask before deciding anything.

  2. Paperwork and your Good Faith Estimate

    Sent securely before the first session, so we don't spend session time on forms.

  3. A secure video link

    No app to install and nothing to figure out. A device with a camera and a place where no one will walk in.

  4. Each session, I confirm where you are

    You need to be physically in Oregon during our session — that's a licensing requirement. It also means I know where to send help if we ever need it.

Practical questions

What people want to know.

Can I do a session from my vehicle?

Yes, as long as it's parked and private. Plenty of people take sessions from a truck. What matters is that no one can overhear and you aren't driving.

What if I'm out of state that week?

We reschedule. My license permits me to work with clients physically located in Oregon, so a session while you're out of state isn't something I can do. Tell me in advance and we'll shift the appointment.

What if the connection drops?

We agree on a backup at the start — usually a phone call — so a bad signal never ends a session on a hard moment.

Do you see clients in person?

No. This practice is fully telehealth throughout Oregon.
