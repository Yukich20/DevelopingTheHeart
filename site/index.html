Beyond the uniform

You are allowed to talk about it.

In a world that rewards productivity, achievement, and constant forward motion, learning to pause can be painful. Slowing down is not simply about physically resting. It means recognizing and addressing the chronic emotional stress that has accumulated over a period of time.

How did something you worked so hard for become so difficult to enjoy? The very qualities that once helped you succeed can also start costing you. Loving your career does not make the difficult parts any less real. You can love the work and still feel its impact on your health, relationships, identity, and life outside of it.

You should not have to wait until retirement to feel like your life is your own.
