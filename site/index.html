Who I work with

High-stress, high-functioning careers — and the people who love someone in one.

Burnout, relationship strain, anxiety, sleep that won't come, anger that arrives faster than it used to. These aren't signs you're not cut out for the work. They're what the work does to people over time.

Most of the people I see don't want to quit. They like large parts of the job. That's exactly what makes it hard to admit when something has started to slip.

  • Nurses & physicians
  • Air traffic control
  • Attorneys & therapists

Spouses, partners, and families are welcome on their own — you don't need the person in the job to agree to it, and you don't need a crisis to justify it.
