FAQs

If what you're wondering isn't here —
reach out, ask me directly.

connect@developingtheheart.com

Do you provide therapy for professionals who work a night shift schedule?

Yes! I offer a dedicated night-shift option on Tuesday nights, from 8:00 p.m. to 8:00 a.m. This is designed with the reality of shift work in mind and is an uncommon option for therapy. So if you've been looking for it, welcome! You finally found it.

As long as you can join from a confidential, private space and are off the clock, we can make it work.

How does it work if I have a rotating schedule?

I work around you, not against you. A rotating schedule can make ordinary routines difficult, but it shouldn't rule out therapy. I don't expect the same appointment time every week from someone whose schedule doesn't work that way.

We can have an ongoing conversation about your schedule, being held over, trades, overtime and everything else that comes with shift work. This is about building something around you rather than asking you to fit a template!

What if I'm not a first responder or high-demand professional?

Welcome, I'm glad you're here!

If you've landed on my website, I believe there's something speaking to you. Trust your gut and book a consultation.

If your work takes a lot out of you, affects your relationships, or leaves you wondering what needs to change, let's connect.

I also work with the people who live life alongside a professional: spouses, partners, friends, adult children, and family members. Your experience matters, too!

Do you work with couples who aren't married?

Yes! Marriage is not a requirement.

I work with couples who are dating, engaged, partnered, living together, or long distance. What matters is that you're both invested in the relationship and willing to take an honest look at what's happening.

What can I expect regarding confidentiality?

Yes, confidentiality is a cornerstone of therapy. I protect your information and do not share it without your permission, except as permitted or required by law. During our first session, we’ll review the informed consent document, including the limits of confidentiality, such as required abuse reporting, serious safety threats to you or someone else, and other legally authorized disclosures. We’ll also discuss how confidentiality applies to my required clinical supervision.

If privacy or career-related concerns are making you hesitate, you’re welcome to bring those questions to our consultation.

Do you take insurance?

No, and it is an intentional choice. When a client uses insurance, certain information is required to be shared in order for billing to take place. I operate a private-pay practice, so I don't bill insurance. My current rates are listed openly on the Fees & Payment page, so you'll always know what a session costs before you book.

Are you able to provide a superbill?

Yes, upon request. I can provide a superbill for you to submit directly to your insurance company if you have out-of-network benefits.

I can't guarantee whether your insurance company will reimburse you, as coverage varies from plan to plan. Therefore, I recommend contacting your insurance company directly to ask about your out-of-network mental health benefits.

What's the first step?

Book a complimentary 30-minute consultation to connect! You'll receive a link to my Google Calendar, where you can choose a time that works best for you. Our conversation will take place via secure video through Google Meet. You'll have the opportunity to ask questions, share more about why you're seeking therapy, and decide whether I feel like the right fit for you before committing to anything.
