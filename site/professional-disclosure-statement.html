Philosophy and Approach: My style of therapy is a collaborative process between client(s) and myself. I believe our experiences influence the ways in which we understand ourselves, relate to others, and engage with the world. Patterns that once helped us adapt can eventually create difficulties or leave us feeling disconnected from ourselves. My role as a therapist is to guide clients in better understanding these patterns while recognizing the strengths they already possess. I use an integrated framework, primarily drawing from Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Solution-Focused Brief Therapy (SFBT), Motivational Interviewing, Person-Centered, Strength-Based, Experiential, and Inner-Child therapies.

Formal Education & Training: I hold a master’s degree in marriage, couple, and family therapy from Lewis & Clark College. In my previous career, I worked as a professional Firefighter/Paramedic and served as a medic in the Oregon Army National Guard.

As a Registered Associate with the Oregon Board of Licensed Professional Counselors and Therapists (Board), I abide by its Code of Ethics. I am supervised by Justin Rock, a Licensed Marriage and Family Therapist, Licensed Professional Counselor. Information regarding my supervision is available upon request.

Fees: The initial 30-minute consultation is complimentary. Individual counseling is $200 for a 60-minute session or $250 for a 90-minute session. Couples/Family counseling for adults, 18 years of age and older, is $250 for a 60-minute session or $300 for a 90-minute session. Payment is accepted by VISA, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, debit cards, or electronic bank transfers (ACH).

As a client of an Oregon Registered Associate, you have the following rights:

  • To expect that a registered associate has met the qualifications of training and experience required by state law;
  • To examine public records maintained by the Board and to have the Board confirm credentials of a registered associate;
  • To obtain a copy of the Code of Ethics;
  • To report complaints to the Board;
  • To be informed of the cost of professional services before receiving the services;
  • To be assured of privacy and confidentiality while receiving services as defined by rule or law, with the following expectations: 1) Reporting suspected child abuse; 2) Reporting imminent danger to you or others; 3) Reporting information required in court proceedings or by your insurance company, or other relevant agencies; 4) Providing information concerning associate case consultation of supervision; and 5) Defending claims brought by you against me; and
  • To be free from being the object of discrimination on any basis listed in the Code of Ethics while receiving services.
