Why I don't take insurance. Billing a claim means a diagnosis on permanent record with your insurer, notes that can be requested and reviewed, and a company deciding how long you're allowed to be in treatment. For people whose careers involve periodic evaluations and background checks, that record can follow you. Paying directly removes all of it.
