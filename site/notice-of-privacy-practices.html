Notice of Privacy Practices

THIS NOTICE DESCRIBES HOW MEDICAL INFORMATION ABOUT YOU MAY BE USED AND DISCLOSED AND HOW YOU CAN GET ACCESS TO THIS INFORMATION. PLEASE REVIEW IT CAREFULLY.

This Notice of Privacy Practices (“NPP”) applies to current, former, and prospective clients whose Protected Health Information (“PHI”) is created or received by Developing The Heart Counseling & Consulting LLC in connection with the health care services it provides. You may have additional rights under Oregon and other applicable law.

EFFECTIVE DATE: September 22, 2026

Under 45 C.F.R. § 160.103, Protected Health Information (“PHI”) means individually identifiable health information, which is: transmitted by electronic media, maintained in electronic media; or transmitted or maintained in any other form or medium.

PHI includes information that identifies you, or could reasonably be used to identify you, and relates to your past, present, or future physical or mental health; health care provided to you; or payment for your health care.

My Responsibilities for Protecting Your Health Information

As a Marriage and Family Therapist Associate practicing under the supervision of Justin Rock, LMFT, LPC, and providing services through Developing The Heart Counseling & Consulting LLC, I am responsible for protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and security of your health information. I will:

  • Use or disclose your health information only as described in this notice, as authorized by you in writing, or as otherwise permitted or required by law;
  • Maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and physical measures to protect your information;
  • Respond to requests to access, review, or obtain copies of your health information as required by applicable law;
  • Notify affected individuals if a breach occurs when notification is required by law; and
  • Follow the privacy practices described in the notice currently in effect.

How I May Collect Your Health Information

I may collect or receive health information when you submit an inquiry form, schedule a consultation or appointment, complete a client intake, sign consent documents, participate in a consultation or therapy session, communicate with me, or provide billing or payment information. With your written authorization, or when otherwise permitted or required by law, I may also receive information from another health care provider, someone legally authorized to act on your behalf, or another person involved in your care.

The inquiry form is intended only for requesting a consultation. Please do not include detailed clinical information or use the form to seek emergency assistance.

How I May Use and Share Your Health Information

I may use or share your health information for the following purposes without obtaining separate written permission each time, when permitted or required by applicable law:

Treatment and coordination of care: To provide, coordinate, or manage your care and communicate with other health care providers involved in your treatment.

Required clinical supervision: As a Marriage and Family Therapist Associate, I practice under the supervision of Justin Rock, LMFT, LPC. Relevant information, including case records, assessments, and treatment plans, may be reviewed with my supervisor as necessary for clinical supervision. Supervision is conducted in a private professional setting and is subject to confidentiality requirements.

Payment: To collect fees, process payments, maintain billing records, issue receipts, and provide a superbill at your request. A superbill is provided directly to you, and you decide whether to submit it to a health plan.

Practice operations: To schedule appointments, maintain and secure records, communicate with you, administer the practice, comply with professional requirements, and obtain necessary administrative, legal, accounting, payment-processing, or technology services. This may include HIPAA-compliant service providers that create, receive, maintain, or transmit Protected Health Information on my behalf. Service providers may receive, maintain, or transmit your health information only as needed to perform services for the practice and as permitted by applicable law.

People involved in your care: To share health information with a family member, other relative, close personal friend, or another person identified by you, but only to the extent the information is directly relevant to that person’s involvement in your care. I may make this disclosure when you agree, when you are given an opportunity to object and do not object, or when I reasonably infer from the circumstances that you do not object. If you are unavailable or unable to express a preference because of incapacity or an emergency, I may make the disclosure when I determine, based on professional judgment and reasonable inferences, that it is in your best interests. Any disclosure will be limited to the minimum necessary.

Purposes permitted or required by law: To make reports required by law; prevent or lessen a serious threat to the health or safety of any person or the public; comply with a legally valid court order; or cooperate with a lawful investigation or other oversight activity conducted by the Oregon Board of Licensed Professional Counselors and Therapists or another authorized agency. Any disclosure will be limited as required by applicable law.

Other legally permitted disclosures: Subject to applicable legal conditions, I may also disclose health information for authorized public-health activities; in response to a subpoena or other lawful process when disclosure is legally permitted and all required conditions have been satisfied; or for law-enforcement, workers’ compensation, or coroner and medical-examiner purposes when permitted or required by law.

Any use or disclosure under this section will be limited as required by applicable federal or Oregon law.

When Your Written Authorization Is Required

Except as described in this notice or as otherwise permitted or required by applicable law, I will obtain your valid written authorization before using or disclosing your health information.

Psychotherapy notes receive additional protection. I will obtain a separate written authorization before using or disclosing psychotherapy notes unless the use or disclosure is otherwise permitted or required by law. Their additional protections are explained later in this notice. Written authorization is also required for uses or disclosures involving marketing or the sale of health information when required by law. I do not sell your health information or use it for marketing.

A written authorization will describe the information to be used or disclosed; identify who is authorized to disclose and receive it; state the purpose of the disclosure and when the authorization expires; and be signed and dated by you or someone legally authorized to act on your behalf. You may revoke an authorization in writing at any time using the contact information provided in this notice. Revocation will not affect any action already taken in reliance on the authorization before the revocation was received.

Information disclosed under an authorization may be redisclosed by the recipient and may no longer be protected by the same privacy laws. Additional federal or Oregon protections may continue to apply to certain mental health, substance-use-disorder, HIV/AIDS, or genetic information.

Your Privacy Rights

You have rights concerning the health information I maintain about you, subject to limitations permitted by applicable law. To exercise these rights, you may submit a written request using the contact information provided in this notice.

You may request to inspect or receive an electronic or paper copy of your clinical and billing records. Access to certain information, including psychotherapy notes and information prepared for legal proceedings, may be limited or denied as permitted by law. I may charge a fee permitted by law and will respond within the legally required timeframe. You may also request an amendment if you believe information in your record is incorrect or incomplete. I may deny the request for reasons permitted by law. If the request is denied, I will explain the reason and inform you of any right to submit a written statement of disagreement.

You may request that I communicate with you in a particular way or at a particular address, and I will accommodate reasonable requests as required by law. You may also request limits on how your health information is used or disclosed. I am not generally required to agree to a requested restriction, but I will honor a restriction when required by law, including certain requests involving services you have paid for completely out of pocket.

You may request an accounting of certain disclosures of your health information. The accounting will not include disclosures excluded by law, such as certain disclosures made for treatment, payment, practice operations, directly to you, or pursuant to your written authorization. You may also request a paper or electronic copy of this notice at any time. You will not be denied services or retaliated against for exercising your privacy rights.

Self-Pay and Private-Pay Rights

If you, or another person other than a health plan acting on your behalf, pay in full out of pocket for a health care service, you may request that I not disclose Protected Health Information relating solely to that service to a health plan. I must honor your request when the information relates only to the service that was paid for in full and the disclosure is not required by law. This right also applies when another person, not your health plan, pays the full cost for you.

Payment by another person does not, by itself, authorize me to disclose your Protected Health Information to that person.

To exercise this right, submit a written request using the contact information provided in this notice and identify the service to which the requested restriction applies.

Developing The Heart Counseling & Consulting LLC does not submit insurance claims or seek payment from health plans. At your request, I may provide a superbill directly to you. You decide whether to submit the superbill to a health plan. If you submit it, you are choosing to disclose the information contained in the superbill to that health plan.

Mental Health Records and Psychotherapy Notes

Mental health information contained in your clinical record—including diagnoses, assessments, treatment plans, symptoms, prognosis, and progress—is protected health information but is not considered psychotherapy notes. Clinical records may be used or disclosed as described elsewhere in this notice.

Psychotherapy notes are notes recorded by a mental health professional that document or analyze the contents of conversations during an individual, group, joint, or family counseling session and are maintained separately from the rest of the clinical record. They do not include medication information, session start and stop times, treatment modalities or frequency, clinical-test results, or summaries of diagnoses, functional status, treatment plans, symptoms, prognosis, or progress.

I maintain “psychotherapy notes,” as that term is defined in 45 C.F.R. § 164.501, separately from the rest of your clinical record. They receive additional protection. I do not voluntarily disclose psychotherapy notes. Applicable law generally requires a separate written authorization before psychotherapy notes may be used or disclosed. Limited exceptions may permit or require their use or disclosure, including use by me for treatment; legally permitted training or supervision; defense in a legal proceeding brought by you; lawful oversight of me as the originator of the notes; disclosures required by law; disclosures to a coroner or medical examiner acting within legal authority; or disclosures necessary to prevent or lessen a serious and imminent threat to the health or safety of a person or the public. Any disclosure will be limited as required by applicable law.

Under HIPAA, psychotherapy notes are excluded from the ordinary right to inspect or obtain a copy of a clinical record. Requests involving psychotherapy notes will be evaluated under applicable federal and Oregon law and professional ethical requirements. Oregon law also protects confidential communications between a client and psychotherapist through the psychotherapist-patient privilege, subject to waiver and limited legal exceptions.

Breach Notification

If I discover or receive notice of an unauthorized acquisition, access, use, or disclosure involving unsecured Protected Health Information, I will investigate and assess the incident as required by applicable law. If the incident constitutes a breach for which notification is required, I will notify affected individuals without unreasonable delay and within the time required by applicable federal or Oregon law.

Any required notification will describe, to the extent possible, what happened; the types of information involved; steps you may take to protect yourself; the actions taken to investigate the breach, reduce potential harm, and prevent further breaches; and how you may contact me for additional information. I will also notify the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services and any other governmental authorities when required by law.

Complaints and Privacy Concerns

If you believe your health information has been handled improperly, your privacy rights have been violated, or this notice has not been followed, you may file a complaint with me using the contact information provided in this notice.

If you believe your rights under HIPAA have been violated, you may also file a complaint with the Secretary of the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services through the Office for Civil Rights (“OCR”). Information about filing a complaint is available at www.hhs.gov/hipaa/filing-a-complaint.

I will not retaliate against you for raising a privacy concern, exercising your privacy rights, or filing a complaint.
