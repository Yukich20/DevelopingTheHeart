What this website collects

This website sets no cookies of its own, runs no analytics, and carries no advertising or tracking pixels. Visiting it does not create a client relationship or a record with this practice.

The inquiry form on the Book a Consultation page sends what you type directly to my Google Workspace account, which this practice uses under a business associate agreement with Google. Nothing is stored on this website, and no third-party form or analytics service is involved.

What you submit is delivered to me by email and kept in a private inquiry log in my Google Drive, visible only to me. I use it to respond to you and to keep track of inquiries I have already answered. Submitting the form does not create a client relationship or a clinical record, and inquiries from people who do not become clients are deleted periodically.

Please keep the form to your name and a good time to reach you. Email is not a secure channel for health information, and you should not include clinical details in it.
