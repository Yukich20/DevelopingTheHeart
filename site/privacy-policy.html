Privacy Policy

At Developing The Heart Counseling & Consulting LLC, I respect your privacy and am committed to protecting your personal information. This Website Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and shared when you visit www.developingtheheart.com or submit an inquiry through the website. It also describes your choices and applicable rights regarding that information.

EFFECTIVE DATE: September 22, 2026

Information Collected

You may voluntarily provide information through this website or by contacting Developing The Heart Counseling & Consulting LLC. This may include your name, email address, telephone number, preferred method of contact, the general reason for your inquiry, and any other information you choose to provide. Providing this information is voluntary, but I may be unable to respond to your inquiry without sufficient contact information.

When you visit this website, the website and its hosting provider, Netlify, automatically receive limited technical information needed to deliver, maintain, and protect the website. This information may include your Internet Protocol (“IP”) address, browser and device type, operating system, referring webpage, pages requested or viewed, date and time of access, and diagnostic or security information. This information does not necessarily identify you by name, but it may be considered personal information under applicable law.

Any information collected through cookies, analytics, or similar technologies is described separately in this policy.

Information Submitted Through the “Let’s Connect” Form

The “Let’s Connect” form is intended only to request a consultation. The form collects your name, email address, telephone number, the service in which you are interested, and any other information you choose to provide. Please do not include detailed clinical or medical information.

When you submit the form, the information is transmitted directly from your browser over an HTTPS connection to a Google Apps Script web application operated through Developing The Heart Counseling & Consulting LLC’s Google Workspace account. The script validates the submission and applies automated spam-prevention measures. It then sends the inquiry to the practice’s email account and stores a copy in a Google Sheet within the practice’s Google Drive. The website’s hosting server does not receive the contents of the form, and no separate form-processing service receives the submission.

Google provides Apps Script, Gmail, Google Drive, and Google Sheets as third-party services. Developing The Heart Counseling & Consulting LLC uses these Google Workspace services under a Business Associate Agreement. Additional information about Google Workspace is provided in the Third-Party Services section of this policy.

By submitting the form, you request that Developing The Heart Counseling & Consulting LLC contact you using the information you provide. Submitting the form does not create a therapist-client relationship, guarantee that services will be offered, or constitute treatment.

To the extent information submitted through the form constitutes Protected Health Information or is otherwise protected by applicable law, it will be handled in accordance with this Website Privacy Policy, the Notice of Privacy Practices, and applicable law.

Do not use the form to communicate an emergency or urgent safety concern. If you are experiencing an emergency or need immediate support, call 911 or 988, or go to the nearest emergency department.

Email and Electronic Communications

Email and other electronic communications are intended primarily for inquiries, scheduling, billing, and other administrative matters. They are not intended to provide therapy or address urgent clinical or safety concerns. Please avoid including detailed clinical or medical information unless we have agreed on an appropriate method of communication.

Developing The Heart Counseling & Consulting LLC uses Gmail through Google Workspace under a Business Associate Agreement. Reasonable safeguards are used to protect electronic communications; however, email and other electronic communications involve risks, including interception, misdelivery, unauthorized access, and storage on devices or accounts outside the practice’s control. Confidentiality and security cannot be guaranteed.

Your use of email and other non-session electronic communications is voluntary. If you contact me electronically or request an electronic response, I may respond using the contact information or communication method you provided. You may request that I use a different reasonable method or stop using a particular method. Because this is a Telehealth practice, electronic technology and certain electronic communications are necessary to schedule and provide services. Relevant communications may be retained and, when appropriate, included in your clinical or business records in accordance with the Data Retention section of this policy. Electronic communications are not continuously monitored and should not be used for emergencies.

Cookies and Analytics

Developing The Heart Counseling & Consulting LLC does not currently configure or place cookies through the website, use Google Analytics, or use advertising pixels or other technologies for behavioral tracking or targeted advertising.

Netlify, the website’s hosting provider, may automatically process limited technical information through server logs and may use cookies, scripts, or similar technologies to provide, operate, secure, or measure the performance of its hosting services. These technologies are controlled by Netlify and are subject to Netlify’s privacy practices. Server logs are records created when a browser requests information from a website and are not the same as cookies stored on a visitor’s device.

Stripe is not embedded in this website and does not place payment-related cookies through this website. If you follow a Stripe payment link sent by email, you will leave this website and enter a Stripe-hosted webpage. Stripe may use cookies or similar technologies on its own services in accordance with its privacy and cookie policies.

If Developing The Heart Counseling & Consulting LLC later adds analytics, advertising pixels, optional cookies, or other tracking technologies, this policy will be updated to describe them, and any legally required notice or choice mechanism will be provided.

Third-Party Services

Developing The Heart Counseling & Consulting LLC uses limited third-party services to operate the website, receive inquiries, schedule consultations, communicate, provide Telehealth services, and process payments.

GoDaddy provides the website’s domain services, and Netlify provides the website’s hosting services. Information associated with these services is described in the Information Collected and Cookies and Analytics sections. GoDaddy’s practices are described in its Global Privacy Notice, and Netlify’s practices are described in its Privacy Policy.

Google Workspace provides Apps Script, Gmail, Google Drive, Google Sheets, Google Calendar, Appointment Schedules, and Google Meet. The use of these services is described in the Information Submitted Through the “Let’s Connect” Form and Email and Electronic Communications sections. Developing The Heart Counseling & Consulting LLC uses applicable Google Workspace services under a Business Associate Agreement.

Stripe provides payment-processing services. Clients receive Stripe-hosted invoice or payment links by email; Stripe is not embedded in this website. When you follow a payment link, you provide payment and billing information directly to Stripe. Stripe may process your name, email address, billing information, payment-method details, transaction amount and status, device information, and information used to prevent fraud. Developing The Heart Counseling & Consulting LLC receives payment confirmation and limited transaction information but does not receive or store your complete payment-card or bank-account credentials. Stripe is not used to store psychotherapy notes, treatment records, or detailed clinical information. Stripe’s handling of information is described in its Privacy Policy.

These providers process information under their applicable agreements, privacy practices, and legal obligations.

How Information Is Used

Information collected through or in connection with this website may be used to respond to inquiries, determine whether requested services are available, schedule consultations, communicate with you, facilitate payment processing, maintain administrative records, prevent spam or fraud, protect the website and practice, and comply with applicable legal or professional obligations.

Information Sharing

Developing The Heart Counseling & Consulting LLC does not sell or rent your personal information. Information may be shared with the service providers identified in this policy only as reasonably necessary for them to provide their services; with professional advisers subject to confidentiality obligations; at your direction or with your permission; or when required or permitted by applicable law, including responding to valid legal process, investigating a security incident, or protecting the rights, property, health, or safety of any person.

If the information constitutes Protected Health Information, its use and disclosure will also be governed by the Notice of Privacy Practices and applicable federal and Oregon law.

Security

Developing The Heart Counseling & Consulting LLC uses reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information collected. These safeguards are designed to protect information against unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration, or destruction and include restricting access and using appropriately secured systems and service providers.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed. If a security incident requires notification under applicable law, affected individuals and governmental authorities will be notified as required.

Data Retention

Personal information submitted by an individual who does not establish a therapist-client relationship will be retained in identifiable form for no longer than one year following the most recent communication related to the inquiry. Information may be retained longer when required by law or when reasonably necessary to establish, exercise, or defend a legal claim; respond to a lawful investigation; or document a security incident.

After the applicable retention period expires, personal identifiers, free-text responses, and other information reasonably capable of being linked to an individual will be deleted or removed from systems under the control of Developing The Heart Counseling & Consulting LLC. The practice may retain aggregated statistical information, such as inquiry totals by time period and service category, for the life of the business. Aggregated information will not include individual submission timestamps or other identifying information, will not be used to contact an individual, and will not be reidentified. When applicable, information will be deidentified in accordance with 45 C.F.R. § 164.514 and applicable Oregon law.

Information incorporated into a client’s clinical record will be retained for a minimum of seven years from the date of the last service, or longer when required by applicable law. Billing and other business records will be retained for the periods required by applicable accounting, tax, professional, and legal obligations.

Information processed or maintained by Netlify, Google, or Stripe may remain subject to those providers’ contractual retention practices and independent legal obligations.

Children’s Privacy

This website and the services offered by Developing The Heart Counseling & Consulting LLC are intended exclusively for adults and are not designed or directed to individuals under 18 years of age. Individuals under 18 should not submit personal information through this website. Developing The Heart Counseling & Consulting LLC does not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 18. If I learn that an individual under 18 has submitted personal information through the website, I will delete the information unless retention is required by law.

External Links

This website may contain links to websites or services operated by other organizations. Developing The Heart Counseling & Consulting LLC does not control the privacy, security, content, or availability of those external websites or services. This Website Privacy Policy does not apply after you leave this website. You should review the privacy practices of any external website or service you choose to use.

Changes to This Policy

Developing The Heart Counseling & Consulting LLC may revise this Website Privacy Policy to reflect changes in its practices, technology, service providers, or legal obligations. Additional notice of material changes will be provided when required by applicable law.
