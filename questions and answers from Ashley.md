Q: Describe a typical first session, plainly
A: Well, 50 minutes is an insurance-decided block of time. I will be offering 60-minute, 90-minute, and 120-min time blocks because I am not reporting to insurance - this gives the client autonomy and choice! First session might be longer because getting a good history is important to make sure we are the right fit and that I am addressing safety issues like (domestic violence, gun safety, trauma history, addiction, etc.) The first session with a couple would look different too as I would split them up within the first few sessions to get their individual perspective on the relationship dynamic and problem. The first session I would hope is really inviting and lots of listening, it's about creating connection so we can get into truth and deep, honest work. Also, I think these interactions start before the first session (ie. website interaction, booking, consultation, intake paperwork) every interaction is a data point.

A: My standard will be once a week (again because I am not taking insurance I could adjust this as needed as some points in life require more support than others if we are growing). I also think people need time to let things sink in and interact with during the week. I would say someone would work with me for 2-5 years

Q: For couples and families: what's your policy on secrets?
A: No-secrets policy — anything told to me individually can enter the joint work

Q: How will you handle it if a couple you see ends up in a divorce or custody case?
A: I will not testify in a custody eval; these can be thousands of dollars, and people try to get a therapist to do this all the time because it is cheaper.
